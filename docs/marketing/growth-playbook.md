# SORZA growth playbook (free)

**The one hook:** *"I let an AI rate my car."* Every car person wants to know what their build scores, and they want to argue about it. Build everything around that moment, and always show the number.

**What's already working for you:** every build link now previews as the car's photo with its SORZA score, and owners have a **Share my score** button under their rating. Every share is an ad.

---

## Your daily routine (about 45 minutes)

1. **Post 1 TikTok, and the same video as an Instagram Reel.** Pick an idea from the list below.
2. **Reply to every comment within the first hour.** Reply with a video to the best "rate mine" comment. Those replies become tomorrow's post.
3. **Send 10 to 15 personal messages** using the scripts below. Never copy-paste the same message to dozens of people: TikTok and Instagram restrict accounts that send bulk DMs.
4. **Rate 5 builds** from people who commented, and post their scores to your story.

Check sign-ups every evening in **Admin → Analytics**. Keep doing the video formats that bring people in, and drop the ones that don't.

## Video ideas (TikTok + Reels)

Keep each one 7 to 20 seconds. The score reveal is the payoff, so build up to it and never show it in the first second.

**Hooks for the first 2 seconds:**
- "I let an AI rate my car and it humbled me"
- "Rating your cars until one gets a 95"
- "This Civic outscored a Porsche"
- "What's holding your build back, according to AI"
- "Stock vs. modded: same car, two scores"
- "Rate my girlfriend's car vs. mine" (your earlier ad, filmed for real)

**Formats:**
1. **The reveal.** Upload a car, then a slow zoom on the score as it counts up. End on the tier (Bronze through Cosmic).
2. **Guess the score.** Show the car for 3 seconds: "Guess before it shows." People comment their guesses, which feeds the algorithm.
3. **Roast the limiting factors.** Read out what SORZA says is holding a build back. Funny and specific.
4. **Before/after mod.** Rate, install a part, rate again. "Did the exhaust actually help?"
5. **Leaderboard drama.** "Someone just passed the #1 car." Show the gap.
6. **Meet rundown.** "Every car at [meet name], rated." Tag the meet's account.
7. **Weekly challenge.** Announce the week's challenge and show the leader.
8. **Your own build log.** Your Mustang's score climbing over time, using the rating history.
9. **Viewer's choice.** "You voted, I rated" using cars from comments.
10. **Category battles.** "Best JDM vs. best muscle car on SORZA."

**Caption template:**
> Rate yours free on SORZA, link in bio. What would yours score? 👇

**Hashtags (pick 4 or 5):** #carsoftiktok #ratemycar #carmeet #jdm #musclecar #stance #modified #cartok #carcommunity #buildnotbought

## Outreach messages

Change at least the first line each time, so it's clearly written to that person.

**Car meet organizer (Instagram DM)**
> Hey! Saw [meet name] last weekend and the turnout was crazy. I built SORZA, a free app that rates builds out of 100 and has a leaderboard. Want to run a "highest score at the meet wins" thing at your next one? I'll feature the winner and your meet on our page. No cost to you.

**Small car creator (5k to 50k followers)**
> Love your [car] content, especially [specific video]. I built SORZA, an app that gives builds an AI score out of 100. Would you rate your car on camera? It usually gets people arguing in the comments. Happy to feature you as a top build too.

**Facebook car group (as a post, only where the rules allow it)**
> Made a free app where you can get your build scored out of 100 and see what's holding it back. Curious what this group's cars score, so drop your SORZA score below. Admins, delete this if it's not allowed.

**Reddit (model subreddits like r/Miata or r/Mustang)**
Read the rules first, since most ban self-promotion. Post your *own* car's rating as a fun post ("AI rated my '66 Mustang 91.25, here's what it said is holding it back") and answer questions about the app in the comments if people ask. Never post the same thing in many subreddits on the same day.

## The meet challenge (your best free event)

1. Ask an organizer to announce: *"Highest SORZA score at the meet gets featured."*
2. Make a small sign with a QR code to sorza.net.
3. Help people rate their car on the spot. It takes about 20 seconds.
4. Film the top 3 reveals for your TikTok.
5. Post the winner and tag the meet. Their friends download the app to see their own scores.

## App Store listing

- **Subtitle (30 characters):** Rate your car. Climb the board
- **Keywords (100 characters):** `rate,score,meet,show,mods,build,garage,jdm,stance,tuner,muscle,club,leaderboard,wheels,turbo,exhaust`
- **Promotional text:** Upload your car and get a score out of 100 in seconds. See what's holding your build back, climb the leaderboard, and find car meets near you.
- **First screenshot:** a score reveal on a great-looking car. Lead with the number, not the feed.
- **Ask for reviews:** at meets, ask people who just got a high score to leave a review while they're happy.

---

## What not to do

- Don't buy followers, likes or reviews. Apple and TikTok both punish it.
- Don't mass DM or reuse one message word for word. It gets accounts restricted.
- Don't rate someone's car on camera without asking them first.
- Don't post a score SORZA didn't actually give. Your credibility is the product.
